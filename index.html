Creamy Garlic Pasta

A simple and delicious creamy pasta made with garlic, cheese, and fresh herbs. Perfect for a quick meal.

⏱ 25 min Cooking Time
🍽 2 Servings
⭐ Easy Difficulty

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup fresh cream
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon fresh parsley
  • 2 cups water

Preparation Steps

  1. Boil water in a large pot, add salt, and cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. Drain the pasta and keep a small amount of pasta water aside.
  3. Heat butter in a pan over medium heat and add the minced garlic. Cook for about one minute.
  4. Add fresh cream and stir continuously until the mixture becomes smooth.
  5. Add Parmesan cheese, salt, and black pepper. Mix well.
  6. Add the cooked pasta and mix until it is completely coated with the creamy sauce.
  7. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve hot.

Enjoy your homemade creamy garlic pasta! 🍝
